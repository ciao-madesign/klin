Klin

Where am I registered?

Type a username you use, and Klin checks on which websites that name exists. Started by you, only when you press the button.

Accounts that appear to exist

    To remove an account, open it and look for the deletion option in its settings. Klin does not delete anything for you.

    Sites that gave no clear answer

    Blocked, slow, rate limited or down. Open them yourself if you want to be sure.

      What this does not do

      • It does not delete accounts. It only finds them.
      • It can be wrong. A site may use the same name for someone else, or answer in a way Klin reads badly.
      • It searches only by username. Not your email, phone number or what data brokers hold about you.
      • Every site you check sees the request, with your internet address and the name you typed. That is the cost of asking. Nothing goes to Klin or anyone else, and nothing is saved when you close this page.
      • Sites are not given your cookies, so you are not logged in and no session is touched.
      • Klin asks your permission to contact websites when you press Check, and you can refuse.

      The list of sites comes from the open-source Sherlock project (MIT licence), fixed at one version and kept inside Klin.
