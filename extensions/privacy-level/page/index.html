Klin

Privacy level

How strongly Klin hides the details sites use to recognise your device. Each level says what it does not hide and what it costs.

Level

What no level does

  • None of them makes you anonymous. Sites see your internet address and your provider sees which sites you contact. Only a tool such as Tor changes that, and Tor is not part of Klin yet.
  • None of them hides what you give away: logging in, filling forms, uploading files.
  • The change applies at once to open pages after a reload; it does not undo what a site already learned.
