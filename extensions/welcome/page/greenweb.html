Klin and Greenweb

Klin reduces. Greenweb measures.

Greenweb is a small extension that estimates the energy, CO₂, water and electricity cost behind your browsing. It is Klin's energy and carbon monitor, and a separate project that also works in other browsers.

What each one does

  • Klin loads less. It blocks advertising and trackers, stops autoplay and avoids connections you did not ask for. Less data moved means less energy used.
  • Greenweb counts what arrives. For each site it notes the site name, the amount of data and the time you spent on it. It turns that into an estimate, shown as a green, yellow or red leaf in the toolbar.

How they fit together

Greenweb is added to Klin unchanged, in the same way as the ad blocker. It keeps its own settings and its own data, on your device. You can remove it like any extension, and Klin keeps working without it.

Greenweb does not need Klin either. It can be installed in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox.

Greenweb is not yet included in this build of Klin. Until it is, you can add it yourself from its project page.

What Greenweb does not do

Greenweb does not measure. It estimates. A browser cannot see what the servers of a site consume, so every figure is a range with a reliability level, and its Methodology page lists each formula and source.

It does not store full web addresses, page content, cookies or passwords. It makes no connection of its own, and it never counts private windows.

Where to find Greenweb

Once it is installed, click the leaf in the toolbar to open Greenweb's own page, with today's figures. This page cannot link to it directly, because one extension cannot open the pages of another.
