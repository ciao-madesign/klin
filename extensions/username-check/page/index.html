Klin

Where am I registered?

Type a username you use. Klin asks hundreds of websites if that name exists there. Only when you press the button.

Accounts that seem to exist

    To remove an account, open it and look for the deletion option in its settings. Klin deletes nothing for you.

    Sites with no clear answer

    Blocked, slow, rate limited or down. Open them yourself to be sure.

      What this does not do

      • It does not delete accounts. It only finds them.
      • It can be wrong. A site may use the same name for someone else, or answer in a way Klin reads badly.
      • It searches usernames only. Not your email, your phone number or what data brokers hold.
      • Every site you check sees the request, with your IP address and the name you typed. Nothing goes to Klin or anyone else, and nothing is saved when you close the page.
      • Sites get no cookies from you: you are not logged in and no session is touched.
      • Klin asks your permission to contact websites when you press Check. You can refuse.

      The list of sites comes from the open-source Sherlock project (MIT licence), fixed at one version and kept inside Klin.
