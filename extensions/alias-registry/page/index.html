Make a disposable address (free, no sign-up)

Klin asks the free service mail.tm for a new random address. Write the site in the form above first, then press the button. You read what arrives right here.

  • Good for sign-ups, trials and anything you can afford to lose.
  • mail.tm can read every message sent to it and may delete the address at any time. Never use it for your bank, your main accounts or password recovery.
  • Pressing the button contacts api.mail.tm with your IP address. Klin asks your permission first.

    What this does not do
    
    
      
      
    • It does not create permanent aliases. It remembers the ones you made with a service you choose. Disposable addresses come from mail.tm.
      • 
      
    • It is kept on this device only, in your Klin profile, not encrypted. Anyone who can open your profile can read it. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no sync.
      • 
      
    • It cannot prove who leaked an address. It tells you who you gave it to. A site can also be breached.
      • 
      
    • The password of a disposable address stays in this browser only and is not in a backup file: after loading a backup its inbox cannot be opened.
      • 
      
    • A backup is a plain file. Keep it where you keep your private files.
