What this does not do

  • It does not create aliases. It remembers the ones you made, with a service you choose.
  • It is kept on this device only, inside your Klin profile, not encrypted. Anyone who can open your profile can read it. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no sync.
  • It cannot prove who leaked an address. It tells you who you gave it to. A site can also be breached.
  • A backup is a plain file. Keep it where you keep your private files.
