Private window

It keeps this computer from remembering what you do here. It does not make you invisible.

What it does

  • Forgets history, cookies, form entries and cached files when you close the window.
  • Keeps what you do here apart from your normal windows.
  • Klin's protections stay on: trackers blocked, fingerprints reduced.

What it does not hide

  • Your internet provider, and the owner of the Wi-Fi network (home, work, school, café): they still see which sites you contact.
  • The sites you visit: they see your IP address, and what you do on them.
  • Your accounts: once you log in, the site knows who you are.
  • What you download or bookmark: it stays on this computer.

To see what a site can read about you, open it and click What can this site see? in the toolbar.
