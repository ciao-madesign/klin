    Catalogues

    A catalogue is a file of services made by someone else: a community, an association, a person. Nothing is loaded until you ask, and you see every domain before anything is saved.

    This contacts that server and it sees your IP address. Klin asks your permission for that one server only, and takes the permission back right after.

        What this does not do

        • It does not rate anyone. Klin gives no score for independence, trust or ethics. You decide with the filters.
        • It is kept on this device only, in your Klin profile, not encrypted. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing connects by itself.
        • It does not check the services or the catalogues. Dates and details are what you or the catalogue wrote. Klin has not verified them.
        • It opens a service in a new tab, the plain way. There is no Klin proxy and no Klin account.
